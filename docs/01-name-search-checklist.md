# LLC Name-Search Checklist

Education only — not legal advice. Verify current rules with your state's business filing office (Secretary of State or equivalent) before you file anything.

## Why this matters

Your LLC's legal name must be distinguishable from names already on file in your state. If it isn't, your formation filing can be rejected. This checklist walks you through a basic name search and the most common conflicts to watch for.

*Asdale Dynamics, LLC is an Indiana LLC and files through INBiz (in.gov/sos/business). Your state's office and website may be named differently — the steps below apply anywhere with the right local site swapped in.*

## Step 2: Check your state's business name database

Search the official registry — not Google, not a domain registrar. Only the state database shows names already filed.

## Step 3: Check for designator and restricted-word rules

Most states require an LLC designator and restrict certain words.

- [ ] Name includes a valid designator: "LLC", "L.L.C.", or "Limited Liability Company" (confirm your state's list)
- [ ] Name avoids restricted words that need extra approval (common examples: Bank, Trust, Insurance, Attorney, University, Federal, Police — varies by state)
- [ ] Name doesn't imply a government agency or a different entity type (e.g., "Corp," "Inc," "LLP")

## Step 4: Optional but recommended checks

- [ ] Domain name: check whether a matching website domain is available (not required to form an LLC, but useful for branding)
- [ ] Trademark: search the USPTO trademark database (uspto.gov) so you don't infringe someone else's brand — a state-approved name can still conflict with a federal trademark
- [ ] Social media handles: check platforms you plan to use

## Indiana-specific notes (Asdale Dynamics, LLC's home state)

- Search at INBiz: in.gov/sos/business → "Search Businesses"
- Indiana requires a designator such as "LLC", "L.L.C.", "Limited Liability Company"
- Name reservation is available through INBiz for a fee and holds a name for 120 days
- "Ltd. Liability Co." and similar abbreviations are also accepted; check the current INBiz naming rules before filing

## Disclaimer

Name availability is determined by the filing office at the time of filing. A search result does not guarantee approval. This checklist is educational only — not legal advice.
