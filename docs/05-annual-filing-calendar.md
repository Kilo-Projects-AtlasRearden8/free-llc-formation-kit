# Annual Filing Calendar

Education only — not legal advice. Keeping your LLC in good standing means filing required reports on time and paying required fees. Deadlines and requirements vary by state — confirm with your Secretary of State and tax agency. This calendar is a starter you can adapt.

## Indiana-specific notes (Asdale Dynamics, LLC's home state)

- Indiana LLCs file a **biennial report** every two years with the Indiana Secretary of State via INBiz — due in the anniversary month of formation, every other year (e.g., a company formed in October 2026 reports in October 2028, then October 2030)
- Biennial report fee: check INBiz for the current amount (online filing is cheaper than mail)
- Indiana does not have a general annual report requirement like some states
- Asdale Dynamics, LLC was formed October 2026 — its first biennial report is due October 2028
- Register with the Indiana Department of Revenue if you collect sales tax or have employees; filing frequencies are assigned after registration

## Tips

- [ ] Put every deadline in your calendar with a reminder 30 days ahead
- [ ] Keep copies of every filing and confirmation number
- [ ] Late reports can bring penalties and, eventually, administrative dissolution
