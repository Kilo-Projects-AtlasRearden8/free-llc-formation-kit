- [ ] Receive stamped/approved Articles (keep with your LLC records)
- [ ] Request certified copies if you need them for a bank account
- [ ] Note the approval date — it starts your LLC's existence and may set deadlines for later steps (operating agreement, EIN, initial report)

## Indiana-specific notes

- File online at INBiz (in.gov/sos/business) or by mail with the Indiana Secretary of State, Business Services Division
- Indiana Articles of Organization require: LLC name, principal office address, registered agent name/address, management type, and organizer signature
- Online filings are typically processed faster than mail filings
- Indiana does not require an annual report; it requires a biennial report filed every other year (see the Annual Filing Calendar in this kit)

## Disclaimer

This template is an educational outline, not a state-approved form and not legal advice. State forms change. Download the current form from your Secretary of State's website before filing, or file online through the state's official portal.
