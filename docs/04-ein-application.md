# EIN Application Steps

Education only — not legal advice. An EIN (Employer Identification Number) is a free federal tax ID from the IRS. You use it to open a business bank account, file taxes, and hire employees. You do not need to pay a third party to get one.

## Before you apply

- [ ] Your LLC is formed (approved Articles of Organization) — apply in your LLC's legal name and formation date
- [ ] You have a responsible party (an individual with an SSN, ITIN, or existing EIN who controls the LLC)
- [ ] You know your LLC's principal address and state of formation
- [ ] You know the responsible party's name and SSN/ITIN/EIN

## How to apply (free, via IRS.gov)

**Option 1: Online (fastest — get your EIN immediately)**
- [ ] Go to IRS.gov and search "Apply for an Employer Identification Number (EIN) Online"
- [ ] Confirm you're eligible: principal business in the U.S. or U.S. territories, and the responsible party has a valid SSN, ITIN, or EIN
- [ ] Complete the online application in one session (it times out after 15 minutes of inactivity)
- [ ] Submit and receive your EIN immediately — save or print the confirmation notice (CP 575)

**Option 2: By fax (about 4 business days)**
- [ ] Complete Form SS-4 ("Application for Employer Identification Number")
- [ ] Fax to the IRS number listed in the Form SS-4 instructions for your state
- [ ] Receive your EIN by fax return

**Option 3: By mail (about 4 weeks)**
- [ ] Complete Form SS-4
- [ ] Mail to the IRS address listed in the Form SS-4 instructions for your state
- [ ] Receive your EIN letter by mail

**Option 4: By phone (international applicants only, no U.S. address/SSN/ITIN)**
- [ ] Call the IRS international number listed on IRS.gov

## After you get your EIN

- [ ] Save the CP 575 confirmation notice — you'll need it for banks and vendors
- [ ] Write the EIN here: ________________________
- [ ] Open a business bank account (bank will want your EIN, Articles, and usually your operating agreement)
- [ ] Use the EIN on tax filings, not your personal SSN, once the LLC is active
- [ ] If you applied by mistake or don't need the EIN, contact the IRS to close the account — an EIN is never reused

## Indiana-specific notes

- Indiana does not issue a separate state tax ID for most LLCs at formation; your federal EIN is used for state tax registration through INBiz when needed (e.g., sales tax, withholding)
- Register with the Indiana Department of Revenue (dor.in.gov) if you will collect sales tax or have employees

This guide is educational only — not tax advice. IRS rules and application procedures change. Check IRS.gov for current instructions. Consider a tax professional if your situation is complex (multiple states, foreign owners, excise taxes, etc.).
