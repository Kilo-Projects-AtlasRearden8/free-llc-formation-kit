4. EIN Application Steps

Apply for a free Employer Identification Number (EIN) from the IRS. You do not need to pay a third party to get one. Education only — not legal or tax advice.

Education only — not tax advice. IRS rules and application procedures change. Check IRS.gov for current instructions. Consider a tax professional if your situation is complex.

Before you apply

How to apply (free, via IRS.gov)

Option 1 — Online (fastest — get your EIN immediately)

Option 2 — By fax (about 4 business days)

Option 3 — By mail (about 4 weeks)

Option 4 — By phone (international applicants only)

No U.S. address/SSN/ITIN: call the IRS international number listed on IRS.gov.

Form SS-4 worksheet (keep this sheet handy)

FieldYour entry
Line 1: Legal name of entity (your LLC’s exact name)
Line 2: Trade name / DBA (if any)
Line 4a–c: Mailing address
Line 5a–b: Physical address (if different)
Line 6: County
Line 7a: Responsible party name
Line 7b: Responsible party SSN / ITIN / EIN
Line 8a: LLC type
Line 8b: Number of members (if multi-member)
Line 9a: Type of entity
Line 9b: State of formation (if LLC)
Line 10: Date business started or acquired
Line 11: Closing month of accounting year
Line 12: First date wages paid (or N/A)
Line 13: Number of employees expected
Line 14: Business activity description
Line 15: Applied for an EIN before?
Third-party designee (optional)

After you get your EIN

Indiana-specific notes

  • Indiana does not issue a separate state tax ID for most LLCs at formation; your federal EIN is used for state tax registration through INBiz when needed (e.g., sales tax, withholding)
  • Register with the Indiana Department of Revenue (dor.in.gov) if you will collect sales tax or have employees
