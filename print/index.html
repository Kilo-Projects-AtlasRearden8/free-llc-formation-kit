How to use this kit

  1. Pick a state. Every page includes notes for Indiana (Asdale Dynamics, LLC’s home state). Swap in your Secretary of State’s website and forms.
  2. Work in order. Name search → file Articles of Organization → write an operating agreement → get a free EIN from the IRS → set up your filing calendar.
  3. Fill and print. Type into the fields, or print blank and write by hand. Browser print dialog strips buttons automatically.
  4. Keep records. Store filings, the EIN confirmation notice, and the signed operating agreement with your business records.
