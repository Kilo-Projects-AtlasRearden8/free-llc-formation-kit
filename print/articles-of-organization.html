Indiana-specific notes

  • File online at INBiz (in.gov/sos/business) or by mail with the Indiana Secretary of State, Business Services Division
  • Indiana Articles of Organization require: LLC name, principal office address, registered agent name/address, management type, and organizer signature
  • Online filings are typically processed faster than mail filings
  • Indiana does not require an annual report — it requires a biennial report every other year (see the Annual Filing Calendar in this kit)
