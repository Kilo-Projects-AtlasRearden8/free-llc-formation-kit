Indiana-specific notes (Asdale Dynamics, LLC’s home state)

  • Search at INBiz: in.gov/sos/business → “Search Businesses”
  • Indiana requires a designator such as “LLC,” “L.L.C.,” or “Limited Liability Company”
  • Name reservation is available through INBiz for a fee and holds a name for 120 days
  • “Ltd. Liability Co.” and similar abbreviations are also accepted — check current INBiz naming rules before filing
